• Names in English: with the English interface, indices, US and Hong Kong stocks, futures and FX show English names (S&P 500, Apple, TENCENT, Spot Gold, EUR/USD), and the menu bar uses short names and tickers such as SSE or AAPL. China A shares and mutual funds keep their Chinese names. Menu bar names you set yourself always win.
• A new starter watchlist in English: the S&P 500 in the menu bar, plus the Nasdaq, Dow Jones, Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, gold and EUR/USD. Existing watchlists are not changed.
• Global indices: add the Nikkei 225, FTSE 100, DAX, CAC 40, Euro Stoxx 50, KOSPI, Sensex, TAIEX, Straits Times and Ibovespa. Search for "nikkei" or "dax"; they are listed under the new Global filter together with futures and FX.
